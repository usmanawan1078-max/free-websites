Fully funded scholarships for Pakistani students, explained in plain language

Last updated: October 2026. Dates on this site carry their cycle year and must be verified on the official programme site before you apply — deadlines change every year.

ScholarshipsPK answers the exact questions Pakistani students type into Google: who can apply, what marks or test scores are needed, what documents to prepare, and when each programme closes. Every guide is written originally for this site, links to the official programme page, and never promises that anyone will win a scholarship. Winning is competitive; this site helps you apply properly.

If you are applying from India, each programme page below also has a For Indian students section. If you are applying from anywhere else, start with our international students overview.

The five flagship programmes

Chevening (UK)

One-year fully funded master's in the UK for future leaders. Needs 2,800 hours of work experience after your degree.

Chevening guide →

Fulbright Pakistan (USA)

Master's and PhD in the USA through USEFP. Needs a GRE score; TOEFL comes later, for shortlisted candidates.

Fulbright guide →

DAAD (Germany)

Development-related master's and PhD courses in Germany. Needs two years of relevant work experience and a strong record.

DAAD guide →

Erasmus Mundus (Europe)

Two-year joint master's across two or three European universities, chosen from the official catalogue. No single deadline.

Erasmus guide →

Türkiye Bursları (Türkiye)

Full government scholarship from bachelor's to PhD, including a Turkish language year. One short window each year.

Türkiye guide →

Start with the programme that fits your level and timeline, then read the IELTS, CGPA and documents guides before you draft anything.
