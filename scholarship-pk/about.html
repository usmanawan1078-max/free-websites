About ScholarshipsPK

Last updated: October 2026.

ScholarshipsPK is a free, independent guide to fully funded scholarships and study-abroad planning, written for Pakistani students first and for international students alongside them. It exists because the information applicants need is scattered across official sites, and the search results around it are crowded with agents, copied content and outdated deadlines. This site tries to be the opposite: one clear page per question, written in plain language, with a link to the official source for every programme.

Who writes this site

Articles are written by Hira Malik, a pen name used by the site's writing team. The pen name is deliberate: the site is about the programmes, not about the people behind it, and no personal details or photographs of any real person appear here. Questions about the content can be sent through the contact page.

Editorial policy

  • Original writing. Every article is written fresh for this site. We do not copy or rewrite competitor pages.
  • Official links. Every programme page links to the programme's official site, and readers are told to verify dates there before applying.
  • Dates carry their cycle year. Deadlines change every year. A date on this site always names its cycle, and pages are updated as new cycles are announced.
  • No paid placement. No programme pays to be listed, and no ranking on this site is for sale.
  • No agents, no fees. We never charge readers, never sell application help, and never recommend paying an agent. Every programme covered here is free to apply to.
  • No outcome promises. Nothing on this site guarantees a scholarship, a visa, or admission. Selection decisions belong to the programmes alone.

What this site is not

ScholarshipsPK is not affiliated with Chevening, USEFP, USIEF, DAAD, the European Union, Türkiye Bursları, the United Nations, or the US Department of State, and it is not an official source for any programme. It does not process applications, hold documents, or contact programmes on a reader's behalf. For anything that decides your application, the official site is the only authority.
