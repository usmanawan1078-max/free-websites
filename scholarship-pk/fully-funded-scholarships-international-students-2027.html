Fully Funded Scholarships for International Students 2027

Last updated: October 2026. Deadlines change every year. Verify every date on the official programme site before applying.

"Fully funded" is the most searched phrase in study-abroad planning, and the most misunderstood. It does not mean every cost in your life is covered, and it does not mean every programme is open to every nationality. The five programmes below are the ones that genuinely cover tuition plus living costs for large numbers of international students, and each has its own eligibility rules, deadlines and application route. Start with the one that fits your level and field, not the one with the biggest name.

The five programmes that matter most

  • Chevening (United Kingdom): one-year master's degrees at UK universities, for future leaders with at least 2,800 hours of work experience after their degree. Applications open around August each year and close in early October. chevening.org
  • Fulbright Foreign Student Program (United States): master's and PhD study in the US, administered in each country by a binational commission or the US Embassy — for example USEFP in Pakistan and USIEF in India. Deadlines and test rules differ by country, so your country's administrator is your real source.
  • DAAD (Germany): the German Academic Exchange Service funds master's, PhD and research stays, including the EPOS development-related master's programmes and the Helmut-Schmidt public policy programme. You apply to the courses directly, not to DAAD. daad.de
  • Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (Europe): two-year joint master's degrees across two or three European countries, chosen from an official catalogue. Each consortium sets its own deadline, most falling between October and January for the following autumn. erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu
  • Türkiye Bursları (Türkiye): bachelor's, master's and PhD scholarships at Turkish universities, with an annual application window that has run from 10 January to 20 February. Age and marks limits apply by level. turkiyeburslari.gov.tr

How to choose between them

Match level first: Türkiye is the only one of the five that funds bachelor's degrees at scale; Chevening and DAAD EPOS are master's-focused; Fulbright covers master's and PhD; Erasmus Mundus is master's only. Then match field: DAAD EPOS suits development-related fields, Helmut-Schmidt suits public policy, and Erasmus Mundus suits students who want a multi-country degree. Then check the calendar: Chevening and Erasmus Mundus deadlines cluster in autumn, Türkiye in January–February, and Fulbright and DAAD deadlines depend on your country or course. Applying to two or three in parallel is normal — just give each application its own tailored essays.

Three warnings before you apply

  1. No one can guarantee you a scholarship. Any agent or website promising a place for a fee is a scam. Every programme above is free to apply to.
  2. Deadlines move. A deadline you read in October may be wrong by January. Check the official site in the month you apply.
  3. "Fully funded" still leaves costs. Visas, initial travel timing, and some personal expenses often fall on you. Read the official terms for what is covered and what is not.

A realistic application timeline

Start at least a year before you want to travel. Months one to three are for research: shortlist two or three programmes, note each one's deadline and test rules, and check whether your degree and experience meet the eligibility bar. Months four to eight are for evidence: book any English or admissions test early, request transcripts and references, and draft your essays programme by programme. The final months are for submission, not for starting: portals close at the stated hour, references take time to arrive, and a rushed application reads rushed. One strong, complete application beats three half-finished ones.
