Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

ScholarshipsPK is a free reading site. It has no accounts, no logins, and no subscriptions, and it does not sell anything. This policy explains what little data is involved in simply reading it.

What we collect

Nothing directly. Reading an article does not require a name, an email address, or any personal detail. If you use the contact form, you provide the name, email address and message you choose to send, which are used only to read and reply to your message. Do not send personal documents, passport details, or passwords through the form.

Analytics

The site may use anonymous, aggregate analytics in future to understand which guides readers find useful — for example, which pages are visited most. Such analytics do not identify individual readers and are not used to build profiles.

Cookies and advertising

The site itself sets no tracking cookies today. In future, the site may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may then use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings. Any advertising partner's use of data is governed by that partner's own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to official programme sites and other external sources. Their privacy practices are their own; this policy does not cover them.

Children's privacy

This site is written for students and graduates applying to scholarships. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the contact form is not intended for use by children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site adds features, such as advertising or analytics. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will change when it does. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
