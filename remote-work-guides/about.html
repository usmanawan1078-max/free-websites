About Remote Work Guides

Remote Work Guides is a small editorial site that explains freelancing and remote work honestly, for beginners. We write about how to start, how to find clients, how to price work, and how to avoid scams — without income promises and without hype.

Our guides are written by a small team with real experience in remote service work, including healthcare billing support and freelance client work. We write under the site's name rather than personal names because the advice should stand on its own merits, not on anyone's identity.

What we believe

  • Honesty over hype. Freelance income varies and takes time. We say so on every page that touches earnings.
  • Original, practical writing. Every guide is written for this site and focused on steps a reader can actually take.
  • Safety first. If a path involves paying to get hired, moving money for strangers, or sharing sensitive data, we call it out.
  • Check the source. Platform rules, fees, and job markets change. We link to official platforms and tell readers to check current terms.

What this site is not

This site is not a job board, a recruitment agency, or a training provider. We do not hire, place, or guarantee work for anyone. Content here is general education, not professional, legal, tax, or financial advice. For decisions about employment, taxes, or regulated work, consult a qualified professional in your country.
