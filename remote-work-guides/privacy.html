Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

What we collect

This is a static informational website. We do not run user accounts, and we do not ask you to create one. If you use the contact form, we receive only what you choose to send (name, email, and message) for the purpose of replying. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the form.

Cookies and advertising

This site may in future display advertising through a third-party network (such as Google AdSense). Such networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalised advertising through the network's own ad settings. Until advertising code is added, no advertising cookies are set by this site itself.

Analytics

If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated to name the provider and what it measures. No analytics provider is active on launch beyond standard, anonymised hosting logs.

Third-party links

Our articles link to external sites (for example, freelance platforms and official resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies before sharing information with them.

Children

This site is written for adults seeking work. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site grows. The "last updated" date above will change when we do. Questions about this policy can be sent via our contact page.
