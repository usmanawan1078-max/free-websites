About Everyday How-To

We answer the tech questions people actually ask — in plain language, with safe steps, and without pretending every problem has a guaranteed fix.

Everyday How-To is written by The How-To Desk, a small editorial team that tests steps on ordinary phones and laptops before publishing them. We use a pen name because the site is about the answers, not about us — and because our readers' questions matter more than our identities.

How we write

  • Safe steps only. If a fix needs you to open a device, touch wiring, or download a dubious app, we won't recommend it. When a job needs a repair shop, we say so.
  • Honest limits. When something only sometimes works — like recovering a deleted photo — we say that plainly instead of promising a miracle.
  • Brand differences acknowledged. Menu names and button layouts vary by brand and model. Where steps differ, we say so rather than inventing one universal path.
  • Original writing. Every guide is written from scratch for this site. We don't copy other sites' content.

What we are not

We are not affiliated with Google, Apple, Samsung, Microsoft, or any phone maker or carrier. We don't sell repairs, we don't offer paid support, and we don't ask readers for personal information. Product and brand names appear only because readers need to know which device a step applies to.

Corrections

Software changes, and menus move. If a step on this site no longer matches your device, tell us via the Contact page and we'll check and update it.
