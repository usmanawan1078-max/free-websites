Privacy Policy

Short version: this site doesn't run accounts, doesn't ask you to sign in, and doesn't sell your data. Read on for the details.

What we collect

Almost nothing directly. This site has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no comment system that stores your identity. If you email us via the Contact page, we receive only what you choose to include in that email, and we use it only to reply or to fix the guide you mention.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertising through Google AdSense in the future. AdSense and similar advertising providers may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ad Settings or your browser's cookie controls. Until advertising code is added, no advertising cookies are set by this site itself.

Basic, privacy-respecting visit statistics (such as which pages are popular) may be collected in aggregate form to help us decide what to write next. These statistics do not identify individual visitors.

Third-party links

Some guides link to official pages run by other organisations (for example, device makers' support pages). Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Children

This site is written for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date below.
