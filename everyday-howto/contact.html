Contact Us

Spotted an outdated step? Want us to answer a question? Tell us here.

We read every message. We can't offer one-to-one tech support or remote help with your device, but corrections and question suggestions directly shape what we publish next.

How to reach us

Email: hello [at] everyday-howto [dot] site (written this way to reduce spam harvesting; replace [at] with @ and [dot] with . when writing to us).

When reporting an outdated step, please include: the guide's title, your phone or computer brand and model if you know it, and what you see instead of the step described. That makes the fix much faster.

What not to send

  • Passwords, verification codes, or account details — never send these to us or to anyone by email.
  • Photos containing personal information (addresses, ID documents, bank details).
  • Payment information of any kind.

See also our Privacy Policy for how we handle any information you choose to send.
